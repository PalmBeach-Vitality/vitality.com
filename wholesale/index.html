Who wholesale peptides are for

The account is for organizations buying research material in volume. Three kinds of buyers use it, and the paperwork is the same for each: a lot number, a COA for that lot, and a packing list that matches the vials in the box.

Research laboratories use the account when a study design calls for the same compound across a series of experiments and the lot should not change in the middle of that series. One lot, with one COA, keeps the analytical record attached to the material that was actually measured.

Research institutions and core facilities use it when purchasing needs a supplier file: where the material was manufactured, which analytical methods the certificate reports, and how the shipment was packed. The order is placed by the institution.

Resellers and distributors use it when they supply research peptides to their own laboratory customers and need bulk vials or kits, lot-level documents, and a replenishment path. A reseller account is for research distribution. It is not permission to make health claims or to present the material as a finished consumer product.

If you are not sure which description fits, send a note through the contact page before you apply. Tell us the compounds, the format, and whether you need one lot held for a program. We would rather answer that before an application than after a shipment.

Volume tiers

Wholesale volume is described in three plain tiers. The tiers are about the order pattern. They are not doses, they are not schedules for use, and they are not a claim about a biological result.

Introductory supply is a first wholesale order for a lab or institution that wants to read a COA, check packing, and qualify a compound before committing to a schedule. New partners are not asked to open at reseller volume. The minimum is confirmed after the account is approved. Starting orders already discussed with new partners run from a few hundred to a few thousand dollars depending on the compound. That range is confirmed in the approval note. This page does not add a public price list.

Recurring laboratory supply is scheduled replenishment for work that is already underway. The point of the tier is the same compound, a documented lot, and a ship rhythm the lab can put on a calendar.

Reseller and bulk supply is ongoing volume for a distributor moving research vials and kits to its own research customers. Private labeling is discussed at this tier, and a standing reserve of an in-stock compound can be requested.

The application asks for an estimated monthly range so we can match a tier. Those ranges are the ones already printed on the form. Pricing after that is quoted to the approved account. We do not post vial prices here.

Bulk research peptides

Bulk research peptides leave here as lyophilized vials and as kits. A kit is a grouped set of vials for a program that needs more than one compound, or more than one vial of the same compound, under one purchase order. Each vial still belongs to a named lot. We do not blend lots to fill a quantity, and we do not relabel one lot as another.

Lot testing is part of the shipment. Every lot ships with a certificate of analysis. The COA is the record for that lot: identity and purity as reported by the methods on the document, plus the lot number that ties the paperwork to the vials. A safety data sheet and batch traceability travel with the shipment. If a purchasing file needs the COA before the purchase order, ask and we will send the current lot document while stock of that lot remains.

Cold-chain shipping is the default for domestic wholesale. Vials are packed for overnight transit so the material is not sitting in a warm warehouse over a weekend. Monday through Friday is the ship window. An order accepted after the daily cutoff, or over a weekend, leaves on the next shipping day. How to hold a dry vial, and how a dissolved analytical sample differs from the dry solid, is covered in the storage guide. That guide is about sample integrity for research. It is not a preparation instruction.

All of this material is for research use only. Bulk format does not change that. We do not supply instructions for use, and we do not describe human or veterinary applications.

Stock that needs a longer lead time is named the same way, in writing, before you are asked to commit.

A COA for every lot

A certificate of analysis for every lot is the rule, not an add-on. “Every order” means the lot in that order has its own certificate. If two lots are required to fill one purchase order, each lot has its own COA, and the packing list says which vials belong to which lot. We do not issue one generic certificate to cover mixed lots.

Read the HPLC guide before you compare a 99.9 percent claim with the chromatogram on a real certificate. Area-percent purity is a specific calculation. It does not, by itself, describe salts, residual solvent, or water. The supplier-evaluation guide lists the red flags that matter here, including a certificate that is not tied to the batch. Use both pages when you compare Palm Beach Vitality with another peptide wholesale supplier.
