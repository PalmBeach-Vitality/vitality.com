Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide

Compound summary

NAD+ is a central redox coenzyme. Research examines NAD+ in redox reactions, sirtuin and PARP enzyme activity, and cellular energy metabolism.

Mechanism focus: NAD+ is a substrate for sirtuin and PARP enzymes and cycles between oxidized and reduced forms in redox reactions.
