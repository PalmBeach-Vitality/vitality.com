Compound summary

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist studied in incretin-receptor research.

Mechanism focus: Tirzepatide engages two incretin receptors, GIP and GLP-1. Research compares its receptor binding and signaling bias with single-receptor agonists.
